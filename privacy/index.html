Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

1. Overview

SpaceReserve ("the App") is designed with privacy in mind. We collect the minimum data necessary to provide and improve the service. This policy complies with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable data-protection laws.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

SpaceReserve stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Venue data: Floor plans, spaces, and their configurations.
  • Booking data: Guest names (optional), party sizes, times, durations, tables, and any notes you enter.
  • Waitlist and staff data: Waitlist entries, and staff names and table assignments, if you use these features.
  • Settings: Working hours, booking defaults, and app preferences.

All data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. No Third-Party Tracking

This build of SpaceReserve does not use third-party analytics, third-party crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or App Tracking Transparency prompts.

  • We do not send app-usage events to third parties.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We do not sell, license, or share your data with data brokers.

4. Support Communications

If you contact support by email, we use the information you provide only to respond to your request and troubleshoot the reported issue.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal data on our servers.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or sell data to advertisers.
  • We do not share, sell, or transfer your booking or guest data to third parties.
  • We do not track your location.

6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, our legal bases for processing data are:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): Local data storage is necessary to provide the reservation-management service you use.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): Processing support requests and maintaining the reliability and security of the app.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): Processing subscription receipts to verify your plan status.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to:

  • Access your data — all booking data is visible within the app.
  • Delete your data — use Settings > Data & Backup > Delete Guest Data (you can also delete saved backups there), or Settings > Reset All Data, which also deletes saved backups.
  • Data portability — export your bookings as CSV from Settings > Data & Backup (available on the Professional and Unlimited plans).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

8. Subscription Data

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive only a transaction receipt to verify your subscription status. We do not have access to your payment information.

9. Local Notifications

With your permission, SpaceReserve sends local notifications about upcoming reservations. Notification data never leaves your device. Notifications name the guest unless you have turned on App Lock, in which case the guest's name is left out so it does not appear on your lock screen.

10. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in the app's database on your device and protected by iOS Data Protection (file protection), which ties it to your device passcode. A flag recording that you completed the welcome screens is stored in the iOS Keychain. App preferences, including whether App Lock is on and your current plan, are stored in the app's preferences on your device. App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.

Backups: automatic and manual backups are copies of your data kept in the app's storage on your device (they are included in your own device backups if you use iCloud or computer backups). CSV exports are created only when you export, and are shared to the location you choose. Backups contain the same information as the app, so delete them if you delete data and want it gone. We recommend enabling a device passcode and biometric authentication.

11. Data Retention

Local booking data is retained until you delete it. Bookings older than your plan's history window (7 days on Starter, 90 days on Professional, 365 days on Unlimited) are hidden in the app but kept on your device until you choose Settings > Data & Backup > Remove Old History; upgrading shows them again. Automatic backups are removed after a limited number of days that depends on your plan. Support emails and any related troubleshooting notes are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the issue and maintain basic support records.

12. Children's Privacy

SpaceReserve is a business tool not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in an updated effective date above.

14. Contact
