Support

If you have questions, need help, or want to report a problem, contact us at ivo.g.sft@gmail.com.

How to Get Help

  • Use Settings > Support > Report an Issue to send a detailed support email from the app.
  • Include the steps to reproduce the issue and what you expected to happen.
  • If relevant, include your app version and device model so we can troubleshoot faster.

Common Topics

  • Subscription and billing questions are managed through your Apple ID account settings.
  • Booking data is stored locally on your device unless you choose to export it.
  • You can review privacy details and data controls in our Privacy Policy.

Privacy of Support Requests

If you contact support by email, we use the information you provide only to respond to your request and troubleshoot the reported issue.
